📌 About The Project

Understanding our vision

AI Academic Copilot is an intelligent educational assistant that combines Artificial Intelligence with modern web technology to create a personalized learning experience.

The platform provides students with digital notes, previous year papers, AI doubt solving, quizzes and performance tracking features in one place.

The goal is to reduce learning difficulties and provide students a smarter way to prepare for exams.
